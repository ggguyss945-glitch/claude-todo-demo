# LAST LIGHT · thumbnail rules (from the channel owner)

Every episode ships 3 thumbnails for YouTube's Test & compare, 3840x2160 (16:9), and ONE of them is always the
niche's split format, exactly like engine/brand/v5/ep2_C_day8_day14.js (ep1: engine/brand/v2/pro.js thumbA4):

* Diagonal split, white seam with an ink outline (SEAM [[1000,0],[920,1080]] in design units).
* Left: "DAY <first day of the episode>" in white; the bright, calm moment (store in daylight, innocent face).
* Right: "DAY <last day>" in red; the same hero later, dark, the dead close behind in colour, serious and banged up.
* From episode 2 on, a "PART <n>" stamp across the seam (red border, red Lilita One, rotated -6°).
* Title pairs with it: "Zombie Outbreak: <N> Days <Trapped/Locked> ... (Part <n>)".

The other two are free concepts from the episode's strongest moments. All three obey:
1. Understood instantly, at the 168-px "Up next" size, with no thinking.
2. Faces serious or innocent and casual; never laughing, cute or goofy.
3. Zombies clearly visible and in colour, never black silhouettes.
4. Professional, clear, organized, catchy; 1–3 huge words of text.
5. Time box: about 20 minutes for the three.
