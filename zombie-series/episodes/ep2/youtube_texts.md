## Description
Day 8. Freshway is split in two. You have the front: fourteen aisles, eight thousand cans. Dale has the back — and every gallon of water you own. When the water stops coming, you have three days.

This week you drain a water heater, learn that the walls have ears, cross a parking lot full of the dead in a thunderstorm, and climb a dead mall toward a blinking light. Someone over there is alive. And Dale has been hiding something since the first night.

What you'll learn in this episode:
• Why city taps go dry a day or two after the power goes out
• Where every building hides clean water (and how to make it safe: 8 drops of plain bleach per gallon, wait 30 minutes)
• How to tell how far away a storm is: every 3 seconds between flash and thunder ≈ 1 km
• Why sound fades about 6 dB every time the distance doubles

Rules so far:
1. They hear. They don't see.
2. Music is a dinner bell.
3. Count the radios.
4. Never tell anyone what you have.
5. The walls have ears.
