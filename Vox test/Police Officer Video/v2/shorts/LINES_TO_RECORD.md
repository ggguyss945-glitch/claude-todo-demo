# New lines to record for the three Shorts

There are 7 required lines and 2 optional ones. Every other line in the Shorts comes from the long video's narration and is cut by line index from `lines.py` (see `SHORTS_PLAN.md`).

Record them in the same TTS voice and settings as the long-video narration (`narration_en_v2.wav`), so the new lines blend with the cut lines. Record each line 2-3 times and keep the best take.

Export the takes as 48 kHz WAV, one file per line, named by ID (for example `S1-N1.wav`). Leave about 0.3 s of room tone at each end; the assembler trims it.

The Russian text is only needed if you also want Russian versions of the Shorts.

## Short 1: THE OTHER GENTLEMAN

**S1-N1** (hook, frame 1, starts at 0.25 s)
> If a cop knocks after midnight... don't prove you live there.
- Delivery: a low, urgent warning, as if leaning in to tell a secret.
  - Quick through "If a cop knocks after midnight".
  - A short catch (0.2 s) at the "...".
  - Hit "PROVE" hard, then let "you live there" drop.
- Target length: 3.2-3.5 s. It must be finished by about 3.6 s.
- RU: Если коп стучит после полуночи... не доказывай, что ты здесь живёшь.

**S1-N2** (subscribe, at about 50 % of runtime, over the palm slapping the car window)
> Everyone who subscribes gets to keep their own face.
- Delivery: half-whispered and fast, like an aside inside the story. Slight smile in the voice on "own face". No pause before it or after it.
- Target length: 2.2-2.6 s.
- RU: Каждый, кто подпишется, сохранит своё лицо.

**S1-N3** (end CTA, over the rule cards flicking to "FULL VIDEO ON THE CHANNEL")
> Seven rules keep you alive. The full video is on the channel.
- Delivery: calm and certain, with a little air after "alive" (0.25 s). The second sentence is plain and clear, not salesy. Do not let the pitch rise at the end, so the loop feels unfinished.
- Target length: 3.4-3.8 s.
- RU: Семь правил сохранят тебе жизнь. Полное видео — на канале.

## Short 2: YOUR VOICE ON THE RADIO

**S2-N1** (subscribe, at about 43-48 %, over you covering your mouth while the waveform goes flat)
> Everyone who subscribes gets to keep their own voice.
- Delivery: whispered and close to the mic, as if you're afraid the radio can hear you. Lean on "own voice".
- Target length: 2.3-2.7 s.
- RU: Каждый, кто подпишется, сохранит свой голос.

**S2-N2** (end CTA, over the mother's house at night)
> Where's your mother? Find out in the full video on the channel.
- Delivery: say "Where's your mother?" quietly, as a real, worried question, then leave a 0.3 s pause. Say the rest plainly and evenly, and end low.
- Target length: 3.6-4.0 s.
- RU: Где твоя мама? Узнай в полном видео на канале.

## Short 3: NEVER CORRECT HIM

**S3-N1** (subscribe, at about 45 %, over a split screen of the double above you)
> Everyone who subscribes knows which one is real.
- Delivery: half-whispered with a sly edge, and a slight stress on "real".
- Target length: 2.2-2.5 s.
- RU: Каждый, кто подпишется, знает, кто из них настоящий.

**S3-N2** (end CTA, over an extreme close-up of the mother's lock)
> Does she open the door? Watch the full video on the channel.
- Delivery: "Does she open the door?" is tense and slow, like holding your breath. Leave a 0.25 s pause, then say the rest plainly and end low (no upward "YouTuber" tone).
- Target length: 3.4-3.8 s.
- RU: Откроет ли она дверь? Смотри полное видео на канале.

## Optional: alternative hooks for an A/B re-upload

Only use these if a Short's 3-second hold rate is weak.

**S2-N0** (replaces line 63 as the second sentence of Short 2)
> This is your voice. On a police radio.
- Delivery: flat and unsettled, with a beat between the two sentences.
- Target length: 2.2-2.5 s.
- RU: Это твой голос. В полицейской рации.

**S3-N0** (said over frame 1 of Short 3, before "What color are the kitchen tiles?")
> He knows everything about you. Almost.
- Delivery: quiet, with a 0.3 s pause before "Almost.", which lands cold.
- Target length: 2.3-2.6 s.
- RU: Он знает о тебе всё. Почти.
