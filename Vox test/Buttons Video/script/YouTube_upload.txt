DESCRIPTION
If your mom's eyes turn into buttons, don't keep her secret.

My mom worked twelve-hour help-line shifts at our kitchen table. Strangers got "I'm listening." I got one finger. So one night, in a game chat, I typed the worst thing I've ever typed: "I wish I had a different mom."

A grey default head answered. It asked me about her: her messy bun, her cardigan, our dented car, our song. With every answer it filled in. Then it asked what color her eyes were, and I didn't know. Two black buttons clicked on where her eyes should be.

When I was eight, my mom and I wrote seven rules inside my closet door. At eleven, I broke four of them in one week. By midnight on my birthday, a police officer stood in our kitchen, my mom was crying my name... and I was standing right next to her.

Online safety in one line: never share your address, school, schedule or photos online, never meet anyone without a parent, surprises are okay but secrets from your parents are not, check first before going anywhere or getting in any car, and if anyone tries to take you, say no, get away and tell a grown-up you trust (and keep telling until someone helps).
