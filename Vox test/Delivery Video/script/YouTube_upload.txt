TITLE
If A Delivery Driver Rings Your Doorbell At 3 AM... DON'T SIGN FOR IT

(alternates)

Thumbnail command: DON'T SIGN


DESCRIPTION
If a delivery driver rings your doorbell at three in the morning and asks for a signature, don't sign for it.

At 3:07 your phone lights up: "Your package has been delivered." You didn't order anything. On the doorbell camera, a driver in a brown uniform is smiling into the lens, and next to his boots is a tall, narrow box wrapped in brown tape. The sender says only one word: RETURN.

Your father walked out on your family twenty-one years ago. That's what everyone said. Tonight, someone is trying to deliver him back. All it takes is one signature from anyone in the house.

There are seven rules. The fourth is the one I broke.
