## Story in short (the script is the authority)
Adam, 12, lonely since his best friend Danny pulled away, finds that a new girl, Evie ("Like forever"), has decided he is her favourite person in the whole world. Danny hands him a card of seven rules in his mom's purple ink "for when someone holds on too tight", one name scribbled out. Evie floods his phone (47 messages in one night), gives him a bracelet, takes his password and his location, turns up beside Mom's car at every red light, sings his secret song, and every night chalks ONLY ME one stretch nearer his green front door. He follows her white sneaker prints to the Keeping Square, the faded red top square of an old hopscotch where you chalk a name and ONLY ME and that person never leaves you, and finds his own handwriting: DANNY rubbed out, ADAM written in. Evie is his own wish, pointed back at him. The scribbled name on the card was ADAM; the 47 messages were his to Danny first; the figure at Danny's blue door in the cold open was him. Mom knew all week (she scrubbed his chalk off the path every dawn). He tells everything, Mom saves, blocks and reports, he says a kind clear no, Evie drains to dust on the doormat, the caretaker paints the square over, Danny explains he crossed the name out himself ("You were still my friend. I just needed room."), and Lina sings the healed song.

## Hard limits (halal and kid-safe; the owner is a Muslim creator)
- No romance of any kind (Evie's fixation is "best friend, only me"); nobody touches anybody (her four limits: she cannot touch; she comes only as close as his yeses let her; she knows only what Adam knows; a grown-up's look holds her back and telling drains her).
- Modest clothes for everyone in every form; no blood, cuts, claws, teeth-baring, cracking sounds, injuries, weapons, deaths. Her smile is a smooth white chalk line, never a cut. She drains to dust, never crumbles into a body.
- The wish is the wrong thing; the fix is real-world (telling, saving, blocking and reporting, the counsellor, a kind no, giving Danny space). Adam is helped, never shamed. Mom, Danny, Lina and Mrs. Rahman are loving, competent and respected.
