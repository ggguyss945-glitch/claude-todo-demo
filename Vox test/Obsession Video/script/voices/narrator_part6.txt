The bracelet in my drawer was chalk dust. The caretaker painted over the Keeping Square.

Weeks later, Danny stopped at my table. Kofi and Sam too.

Danny slid over a shoebox. Every gift I'd given him. On top, the real bracelet.

He'd kept them all.

Then the card. The scribble... in his chewed blue pen.

He's not my only friend. He's one of my best.

Last week, Danny didn't answer all day. My fingertips went white. That old pull.

I put my phone down.

Next morning, one chalk word on my path.

Only.

I scrubbed it off, the way Mom did.

Then I called Danny.

He picked up on the first ring.

I only call once.
