Rule number five.

A real friend is happy you have other people. Never let anyone pull you away from your family.

Saturday dinner.

Behind Mom, outside the dark window, Evie's lips moved. My phone buzzed with every word.

Mom put her fork down.

I pulled my phone into my lap.

Lina held out a drawing of us. I snapped at her and went upstairs.

I typed it. And I heard myself.

I threw Danny's card on the landing.

Only me moved onto our doorstep. Right against our green front door.

Lina knocked. I didn't open.

I looked down.

Evie's neck was bending. Up... and up... toward my window. Her chalk smile almost touched her ears.

Rule number six.

If someone says their feelings are your fault, they're not. You're allowed to leave.

Midnight.

From the landing, I heard our letterbox lift.

Chalk-white fingers slid through. Far too long. Creeping across the hall floor, writing...

O. N. L. Y.

Through the slot, a smile, ear to ear. Humming my song. Slow. Flat.

A chalk stub dropped through, rolling to the bottom stair.

My feet moved. One step down. Two. Halfway.

Behind me, the landing light clicked on.

Lina, under the bare bulb, held up Danny's card. Light showed through the scribble.

Adam.

The rules weren't for me. Danny's mom wrote them to protect Danny.

From me.
