[intense, tense] That night, at three, I woke up.

[intense, almost saying it] "Night, Dad," I almost said.

[dread, intense] And then, from down the hall, through my parents' open door... I heard my dad snore.

[terrified, intense] The man in the doorway stepped into our room.

[horrified, slow] The moonlight hit his face. Long, droopy ears. Heavy, sad eyes. A wet, black, rubber nose. And under it, a mouth that wasn't rubber at all. Human teeth.

[disturbed, gentle] He knelt down beside Theo's bed. And he tucked Theo in. Gently. Like he'd done it a hundred times.

He whispered.

[terrified, very slow] Then the dog face turned, very slowly, toward me. And he lifted one finger to where his lips should be.

[shaken] He stood up, and he went into my closet. I heard the back wall of my closet slide. Then nothing.

[steady, determined] I did not scream. I did not follow him. I didn't look for him.

[urgent, intense, fast] I picked Theo up, blanket and all, with my hand over his mouth, and I walked down that hall, past the closet, where the door had opened just a crack. Just enough to watch me go.

[tight, controlled] I didn't look. I kept walking.

[fast, urgent] Mom and Dad's door. I slipped in, shut it, locked it, and shook my dad awake.
