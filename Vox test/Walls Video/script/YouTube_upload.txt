DESCRIPTION
If a man in a dog head lives in your walls, don't look for him.

We moved into 14 Larkspur Lane in an August heat wave. The old owner, Mr. Pell, "just walked away." He left his green armchair, a stone frog with a key under it, his old garage code, and a note that explained every noise in the house away: the pipes, the mice, the switch that does nothing, the sealed attic.

My little brother Theo was six. He said a man called Hushabye lived in the wall and had a sad dog face. He made me write down seven rules in his dinosaur notebook. Every grown-up in our house had an explanation for every one of them.

Every explanation was wrong.
