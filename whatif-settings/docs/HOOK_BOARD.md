# Hook board: the first 60 seconds (S00 + "Day one.")

Source of truth for shots, sound and captions in the hook. Times are estimates: the timeline builder lays the voice lines end to end with the gaps
below, then every shot is anchored to a line or a word. Picture leads the sentence by 0.1-0.5 s (bible 1.2). Every shot moves (push 2-6% minimum).
Faces change every ≤2 s on the stressed word. SFX target ≥40 in the minute, ≥50% of cuts with a transient within ±0.1 s.

Structure (devices from the reference, our own content): open on the ending → the viewer is the culprit → rewind → the 2:13 a.m. app → the
irreversible press with a sub boom and a ripple that scales outward → first proof gag → snap out to the planet → DAY 1.

Legend: **N** narrator, **SYS** settings voice, cap = caption chunks (`{WORD|colour}` = keyword colour), ⟂ = music drop (hard cut, slam back on the next downbeat).
