# EP01 "Never Move Grandpa": full-episode storyboard (0-113.30 s)

The whole 1:53 voice-over cut into 97 shots in the style of the approved 5-second test, following `docs/STORYBOARD_RULES.md` (the edit) and `docs/VISUAL_BIBLE.md` (the look). This file, `timeline.json` and `ASSETS.md` are generated from one shot table: edit `episodes/ep01/tools/shots_ep01.py` and run `python3 -I episodes/ep01/tools/make_board.py` from `roblox-shorts/`.

| File | What |
|---|---|

| `episodes/ep01/ep01.js` | Timeline entry: re-exports the routed builder map of `engine/episodes/ep01/index.js` |
| `engine/episodes/ep01/index.js` | Loader: reads the timeline, routes each `shot` key to its owner part, falls back to a labelled placeholder (stub) |
| `engine/episodes/ep01/part1.js` .. `part5.js` | One module per parallel animator. part1 holds the approved test builders, unchanged |

## 2. Decisions and changes against the rules, the script and the test

1. **Shots 1-5 are the approved test, unchanged** (builders copied verbatim into `part1.js`, params copied from `test5_timeline.json`, same names s01-s05 and the same frames 0 / 55 / 89 / 126 / 175). Two things cannot stay as in the test file: the test ended s05 at 3.767 s and then held a silent tail (s06, 3.77-5.00 s) because its audio stopped after "gnomes". In the episode the voice says "First, your grandma gets shorter" from 3.59 s, so s05 ends at the cut before "First" (frame 214, 3.567 s, 0.65 s long instead of 0.85 s) and the test's silent-tail builder `lawn_stare` moves, unchanged, to "Nobody knows why they face the house" (s09), where its picture (the family of gnomes staring at the house) is the literal image. Keeping the test's 5.00 s would put a 177 ms late cut and the wrong picture on the grandma line (R1, R2, R3).
2. **The opener is the approved night opener**, not STORYBOARD_RULES 6.1's dusk deck chair. The loop therefore closes on night: the epilogue goes from dusk (the photos, 71, the granddaughter) to blue hour/night on the last sentence, and s97 pushes into an ECU of the old narrator's face framed like frame 0's gnome face (warm window spill from behind the lens, galaxy sky, TF moon behind the hat). The colour script below replaces the 6.1 table's first 26 s.
3. **Human Grandpa is the test's Grandpa** (`grandpa` cast: navy cardigan, white walrus moustache, round glasses, tartan slippers), not the script's olive fishing-hat flashback costume: the approved porch shots and s04 already introduced him, and his navy cardigan matches his gnome's navy coat. The fishing hat stays with the narrator at 71 (`heroOld`).
4. **The world montage keeps the grade switching per sentence** (night opener, day interior, dawn, night, day, dusk, day, day, night...), as the rules' "world rules" section asks. "Nobody knows why they face the house" is night (the approved `lawn_stare`), so its whole sentence is night (R10).
5. **"Then, the night before we moved, / at nine"** switches from dusk to night on the clause "at nine" (the spoken time cue), as the rules' own colour script does. This is the one time-of-day change inside a sentence.
6. **Rack focus at line 42 (script)** becomes a whip pan from the hero's face to Theo's window (s60), per bible section 9 (no DOF in any reference).
7. **"Take Grandpa, and Theo's window goes DARK"** is shown literally as a what-if: the hero steps toward the truck, the light leaves the bed and the window snaps off on "dark". He steps back off-screen between the shots; the window is lit again from the next shot on (see continuity).
8. **"Mom & Dad laugh behind the kitchen glass"** (script line 16) is cut to keep the rate inside 52 per minute; "he'd say" plays on Grandpa's wink instead.
9. **Next door's dog is shown alive once** (s12, chewing a plastic gnome's hat) so that s13 (empty kennel, lead into the hole) lands. It is never shown going into the hole.
10. **The 4 planned pauses** are held inside running shots (R8): "empty" (s27), "fence" (s43), "flowerbed" (s73) and before "Every spring" (s89). "edge" also has a 0.7 s gap, held in s39.
11. **Gnome size** stays 2.0 studs (bible), held in the arms at chest height; the moles' "BIG ones" snout is a per-shot scale cheat x1.8 (bible 2.6 #6).

## 7. Hard to show (and how this board handles it)

- **"Nobody knows why they face the house" / the rule**: Facing the house reads only when the gnomes face the lens (frame-0 pull-back) or we see their backs with the house beyond (lawn_stare). Both are used; the mid-sentence grade stays night.
- **"Houses here come with ancestors"**: An abstract joke. Shown as text on the agent board (pushed into the text on the word) with two gnomes posing as agents; it doubles as the SOLD plant.
- **"You never step on a lawn after dark"**: A rule about not doing something: shown as the sign plus a kid hopping stones and the street's curtains snapping shut on "dark".
- **"Their dog went down a hole"**: Dark beat for a kid audience: the dog is seen alive in s12, then only the empty kennel and the lead into the hole (one tug). Never the dog in the hole.
- **"Take Grandpa, and Theo's window goes dark" / "Leave him, and I never get another blink"**: Two hypotheticals. The first is shown literally as a what-if (step, light leaves, window off) and undone off-screen; the second is shown as its opposite (the gnome looking up, NOT blinking). Risk: a viewer may read s46 as real. Mitigation if the owner wants it: desaturate s46 10 % as an "imagined" look.
- **"He was trying to come WITH me"**: Intent of a statue: shown by his position (at the gate, facing the truck, boots on the line) plus the dewdrop tear on "with".
- **"He was checking on ME" (the twist)**: Needs the earlier plants to land: the lap kink (s22) and the spot under the window (s16) are both shown; the callback re-uses s20-s22 exactly and then cranes down into the kink (s64).
- **"For 60 years, whoever was small in that room"**: 60 years in 2 seconds: B&W Dad (s67) -> sepia 1990s girl (s68) -> Theo now (s69), the same window and the same light source each time.
- **"I wasn't small anymore"**: Size is relative: shot from inside the room so the hero's face fills the window and Theo is small at the bottom (s71). No mirror-reflection effect needed.
- **"like he was SORRY"**: A statue cannot frown: the slow blink plus lids settling low (0.3) on "sorry", lantern under-light, close push.
- **"every gnome faced its house, except ONE"**: Needs the gnome's head turned at the truck and turning back: tele shot from the truck with the head turn after "one", held through the pause.
- **"Theo's sent me a photo" / photos over 60 years**: The photo images are rendered scenes (render-to-texture once per worker), so Theo, the gnome's moss and the window can age year by year. Biggest new-tech item of part 5.
- **"I'm 71" (the reveal that the narrator was old all along)**: The voice changes to the old voice at 109.25 s; the first old image is his hand at 104.93 s (phone pull-back) so the reveal is prepared, then the punch-in on "71".
- **The loop**: The approved opener is a night gnome ECU, not the script's dusk deck chair, so the last shot pushes to a face ECU at night framed like frame 0. It matches size, position and light, not identity (frame 0 is a neighbour's purple-hat gnome). Optional owner decision: swap frame 0's gnome for the `narrator` gnome preset (fishing hat, mustard coat) to make the loop literal; that would change an approved shot.
- **Story logic: our lawn's family gnomes**: The approved s06 put 5 family gnomes on our lawn; with lanterns lit, why do the moles still reach Theo's window? Handled by small pools (radius 2) and keeping them off the bed (continuity table); worth a line in the owner review.
