# KEEP OFF THE LAWN (idea #2 "Grandpa Gnome"): full script, draft write-2

**Source:** idea #2 in `docs/IDEA_BANK.md` (Grandpa Gnome, 69/80), built on the 12-beat 2:15 master in `docs/SCRIPT_FORMULA.md`. The rule, cast, plot and every line are our own. The five references supply mechanics only.

| | Master (2:15) | With the 2:30 stretch lines |
|---|---|---|
| Spoken words | **434** | **479** (+45 in 3 optional lines, S1-S3) |
| Read at 200 wpm, + 4 planned pauses (3.2 s) | **2:13** | 2:27 |
| Read at 192 wpm (formula's planning pace) | **2:19** | 2:33 (trim S3 or speed up 1.05x for 2:26) |
| Read at 180 wpm (slow human read) | 2:28 | 2:43, too long |
| At the references' 257 wpm (synthetic speed) | 1:45, too short | 1:55 |

**Why the word count follows the formula and not the references' pace.** The references run at 257 wpm, a speed no human voice keeps up with. Writing for that speed would mean 515-640 words, and the owner's own read would then land at 2:40-3:20. The formula's budget for a human read is 420-450 words. That gives 2:13-2:19 here, and in the edit a 1.05-1.10x speed-up can still be applied.

**Markup:** CAPS = stress word (also the caption hit). `/` = 0.3 s breath. `//` = planned 0.6-1.0 s pause (there are 4). **[NAME]** = a quoted line. The narrator acts every voice; the captions are colour-coded by speaker. Times are estimates at 192 wpm.

**Voice:** an excited 11-year-old telling it straight, past tense from line 16. Don't age the voice until line 60 ("60 springs ago"). From there, read about 10% slower and warmer: the kid was a 71-year-old all along.
