# Niche notes: "In this world..." Roblox animation shorts

Source: a creator's breakdown of their own channel (transcript pasted by the owner). Our own notes, condensed.

## What the channel reports
- About $5,500/month from under 40 shorts and 75M+ views; Shorts RPM about $0.35 per 1,000 views, so the format must pull millions per short.
- Pivots: Roblox rant lists over gameplay (millions of views, not monetised) → real 3D Roblox animation with characters and a story (6M on the first) → the "world rule" format (biggest short 8.2M) → longer shorts of 2:00-2:30 (two past 5M in one month).
- They won by changing the format, not by posting more.
- Upcoming YouTube Shorts thresholds they mention: 20M Shorts views in 90 days for new channels to join the partner programme, 10M in 90 days to be paid from Shorts. A format that gets millions per short is what clears it.

## The format, in their words, reduced to four steps
1. **First three seconds:** one impossible rule about the world, stated in one sentence on the first frame. Their biggest short opens with "In this world, moving is deadly."
2. **The consequence:** how everyone lives because of that rule (what it means for friends, trust, daily life). This makes the world feel real.
3. **One person:** zoom into one person who is about to test the rule.
4. **The twist at about 30 seconds:** something that changes the whole story and makes people need to know what happens next.

"The rule makes people click. The person makes them care. The twist makes them stay."

## How they write
- The idea (the rule) comes from a person, not a machine. A model helps map the story in four parts with short sentences and punchy narration, then they change a line or two.
- Word-by-word captions, sound effects, narration, posed and animated 3D Roblox characters.
- The longer 2:00-2:30 versions keep the same skeleton and add story beats after the twist.

## Our extra observations from the references (to be expanded by the analysis)
- Titles are short and mysterious ("Never Be Caught Awake At Night", "Porch Night", "Uh Oh", "The Tooth Fairies Project", "Clown Gate").
- Visual style: real 3D Roblox Studio scenes, blocky Roblox avatars mixed with cute-creepy creatures, soft dreamy lighting, a single large caption word at a time in the centre.
